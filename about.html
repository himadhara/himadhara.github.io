Interests

Apart from being a cybersecurity and digital forensics researcher, I enjoy spending time outdoors and connecting with nature. When indoors, I follow numerous sci‑fi and fantasy movies and TV shows. I spend my free time exploring the latest advancements in cybersecurity and the FOSS domain, while also reading self‑improvement books. In quieter moments, I enjoy water‑colour painting as a way to relax and express creativity.

Hobbies

I enjoy attending technical conferences and events, and occasionally giving lectures in my field of expertise. Volunteering in community events is something I value, as it allows me to connect with people and contribute meaningfully. Astronomy fascinates me, and I often spend time exploring the wonders of the night sky.

Gaming is another interest — I particularly enjoy RPGs and strategy‑oriented games, though I also find joy in puzzles such as jigsaw and logic challenges. Walking is a daily habit, giving me time to reflect and think about possibilities. Photography is a quiet passion, where I capture meaningful snapshots of nature and landscapes, not for social media but for personal reflection.

Reading has been my most persistent hobby, while art remains a growing interest — I am still learning abstract painting, craft making, and digital art. Programming is both a hobby and a skill, with Python, Bash, HTML, and UI/UX design being areas I explore. Writing is another outlet, where I often quote my own philosophy to overcome hardships and maintain perspective.
