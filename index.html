Hi, I'm Himadhara Puthran S O. I am an electronics, cybersecurity and digital forensics researcher with experience across hardware security, embedded systems security, IoT security, cybercrime investigation, and device forensics. I have worked as a Multimedia Forensic Expert at National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU), completed internships at Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) Technopark, and apprenticeship at Vikram Sarabhbhai Space Centre (VSSC-ISRO), focusing on cyber forensics, embedded systems, and hardware security.

I hold a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering and an M.Tech in Cyber Forensics and Information Security from APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU). My research interests include ICS/OT, CPS, IIoT security, automotive forensics, hardware security and device forensic analysis. I have published in the IoT-IoV-Automotive Forensics domain and continue to pursue advanced research in cyber-physical systems security and hardware security.
